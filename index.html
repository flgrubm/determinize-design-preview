Two design directions for the determinize site

Each direction has a landing page and a simulator. Open the landing page; its "Simulator" link leads to the simulator, which has a strip of example states at the top. The simulator's theme menu switches between light and dark; the landing pages follow your system setting.

A, the paper's figure
The site continues the paper: its typefaces, its two-listing figure and its plot colours, with one accent for what determinization changes. Distributions are histograms.
B, given the G draws
The site is built around one plot: each run's output against its G draw. The source's runs scatter; the determinized program's runs lie on the curve of the source's mean, so the scatter around the curve is the variance that determinization removes.

These are static mockups with real programs and statistics from the paper and the Lean command-line tool; nothing runs a program.
